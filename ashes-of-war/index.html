Elden Ring Ash of War poise damage

The hardest single hit of 69 equippable Ashes of War. Ash poise scales with the base poise of the weapon it is on, so each Ash shows a range.

Find a weaponAll weaponsBossesAshes of WarPoise vs stance

Ash of War poise comes from the game's shared Ash of War attack table:

poise damage = flat + weapon base poise × attack multiplier ÷ 100

"On the weakest weapon" uses a weapon with base poise 1, and "on the strongest weapon" uses base poise 7.5. The game data lists each hit definition for a skill but not which ones happen in one use, so these are single-hit figures, not totals. Pick a specific weapon in the interactive finder to recalculate.
