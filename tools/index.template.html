How poise damage is worked out.

Each player attack in the game data has two poise fields: a flat amount and a percentage multiplier. Each weapon has a base poise value. The table uses:

poise damage = flat + weapon base poise × multiplier ÷ 100

Most player attacks have a flat amount of 0, so the multiplier does the work. Upgrade level does not change base poise in this game version (every upgrade row has the same poise rate).

Move groups on the Weapons tab come from each attack's behavior ID. The ranges are a best-effort grouping and the raw ID is always shown, so check it against the move you care about.

Frame speeds come from the player animation timing files (TAE) for each weapon class. Each attack's hit window is matched to the move by its behavior ID. Frames are 30 per second. Two weapons have no timing data, and attacks tied to a particular weapon's own animations may show n/a.

Source: regulation.bin from your install. Names from the community Paramdex. Tables read: EquipParamWeapon, ReinforceParamWeapon, BehaviorParam_PC, AtkParam_Pc, Bullet, EquipParamGem, NpcParam.
